Welcome to Kyoto

Kyoto, once the capital of Japan, is a city on the island of Honshu known for its classical Buddhist temples, gardens, imperial palaces, Shinto shrines, and traditional wooden houses.

Rich Cultural Heritage

Kyoto is home to over 1,600 temples and countless cultural treasures. From the iconic Fushimi Inari Shrine to the serene Zen gardens of Ryoan-ji, the city offers a glimpse into Japan’s spiritual and artistic traditions.

Nature and Seasons

Kyoto’s natural beauty changes with the seasons. Cherry blossoms in spring, lush greenery in summer, vibrant foliage in autumn, and peaceful snow scenes in winter make it a year-round destination.

Traditional Cuisine

Kyoto cuisine emphasizes seasonal ingredients and delicate flavors. Try kaiseki dining, matcha sweets, and street food at Nishiki Market for a true taste of Kyoto.
